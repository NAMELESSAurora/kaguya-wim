## 1. Plot outline by act

**1 · Prologue: Iroha's life (0:00:06–0:04:49).** Yachiyo narrates, opening with the first words of 竹取物語 (今は昔). She corrects herself to "a slightly-future world", and the TSUKUYOMI title appears at 0:00:49. Iroha is an ace gamer and a model student. She fell out with her mother, moved to Tokyo alone, and works part-time to pay her own rent and tuition. Exhausted on the walk home, with Yachiyo's debut song "Remember" playing, she cries. A shooting star passes (0:04:05) and she wishes for money.

**2 · The gaming utility pole (0:04:50–0:09:15).** Yachiyo's narration parodies the glowing-bamboo opening: a seven-colour "gaming" utility pole with light inside it. A baby comes out. Iroha says she has her hands full and walks away, then comes back and takes the baby home. The baby cries until a neighbour bangs on the wall. The "Remember" chorus puts her to sleep. Iroha phones the police, hangs up, and spends ¥13,243 on diapers.

**3 · Kaguya grows; the bad ending (0:09:20–0:16:20).** By morning the baby is a girl demanding milk, and she grows again each day. Over omurice at 2 a.m. she says she came from the moon because life there was boring. Iroha shows her the 竹取物語 picture book (0:12:28): the envoys, the feather robe, forgetting Earth. Kaguya calls it a bad ending and says she will make her own happy ending, together with Iroha. Iroha says a "normal ending" is fine.

**4 · Naming and house rules (0:16:20–0:23:30).** At school Iroha aims for the University of Tokyo to satisfy her mother. Kaguya follows her to a café in Iroha's clothes, and Iroha names her "Kaguya" on the spot (0:19:31). Kaguya spends ¥124,400 from Iroha's account, then cooks her a feast. Iroha lets her stay "until they come to pick you up", with three rules (0:22:31).

**5 · Into Tsukuyomi; the Yachiyo Cup (0:23:37–0:35:12).** They dive into Tsukuyomi. Yachiyo gives a tutorial and Kaguya gets a blonde "gyaru" avatar. At Yachiyo's beach mini-live (0:27:17–0:31:06), Yachiyo announces the Yachiyo Cup: the streamer who gains the most new fans in a month wins a collab concert with her. Black Onyx make a showy entrance. Kaguya vows to win. Yachiyo hands Iroha something she dropped and thanks her by name (0:35:06), though Iroha never told her it.

**6 · Streamer and producer (0:35:16–0:47:00).** Kaguya starts streaming with ¥100-shop gear. Iroha shows her chords on the keyboard, with flashbacks to her parents. Kaguya sings one of Iroha's old songs and makes her "Iro P", her producer (0:39:26). A ranking montage runs from 8910th to 3006th (0:40:09–0:41:41). KASSEN game streams follow. Chat floods with marriage proposals, and Kaguya answers: "beat Iro P and I'll marry you" (0:45:33).

**7 · Fever, then the challenge (0:47:36–0:57:01).** Iroha collapses with a fever. Kaguya nurses her and learns about the deal with her mother. Kaguya calls the mother crazy. Black Onyx lead the rankings. Mikado sends a "Taketori-style KASSEN" challenge together with a marriage proposal (0:53:52–0:54:08). Mami faints at the venue, and Yachiyo joins as their third player.

**8 · The KASSEN match (0:57:03–1:10:36).** Black Onyx win round 1. Mikado unmasks Iroha and turns out to be her older brother (0:58:53). Kaguya wins round 2 with an attack from above that the 2D map has no height data for (1:03:19). In a flashback, Iroha's brother leaves home and she is left alone with their mother (1:04:25–1:05:09). In round 3 Iroha learns to rely on her team, but a mine trap lets Mikado win (1:10:28).

**9 · Winning the Cup anyway (1:10:40–1:18:00).** The Yachiyo Cup goes to "Kaguya/Iro P" on fan count (1:11:37) and the crowd chants their names. Mikado drops the marriage idea. Iroha's request is that he sign as guarantor for a bigger flat. Yachiyo breaks the fourth wall: hard turns are coming (1:14:24). The two move house to "Happy Synthesizer". Kaguya finds an unfinished WAV file, "Untitled (collab with Iroha)".

**10 · The collab concert and the first intrusion (1:18:16–1:27:30).** Before the show, Yachiyo says "Remember" has already reached the person it was for. On stage: **World is Mine (1:20:22–1:22:06)**, then the new song "Ex-Otogibanashi". Afterwards Kaguya tells Iroha she loves her and jokes about marrying her (1:24:08). The network cuts out, moon people grab Kaguya on stage, and someone sends them off (1:24:39–1:25:42). TV news reports a Tachikawa outage, "radio-wave hijacking" and images of the moon on digital signs.

**11 · Fireworks and the full moon (1:27:30–1:39:21).** At a summer fireworks festival in yukata, Kaguya says the moon has no taste and no temperature. She asks Iroha whether she loves her mother. Then she admits she must go back: the envoys will come to Tsukuyomi at the next full moon (1:33:22). She chooses to enjoy every day until then and hold a farewell concert. Friends and Black Onyx promise to fight. Iroha plays her first song, the one she wrote with her late father, and Kaguya writes lyrics for it. Iroha then realises its melody is the same as Yachiyo's "Remember" (1:38:58–1:39:13).

**12 · Graduation concert and departure (1:39:22–1:49:45).** Backstage Kaguya gives Iroha a gift, and Kaguya and Yachiyo say goodbye (1:40:23–1:40:46). Kaguya gives a farewell speech. Friends and Black Onyx crash in to hold off the envoys. Kaguya sings her graduation song, then "Reply", saying in the middle that she fell for Iroha at first sight (1:44:57). She welcomes the envoys, says "Iroha, I love you" and leaves (1:47:40–1:47:47). Alone at home, Iroha lists Kaguya's faults, then receives a bank transfer from her: "all the money I owe you".

**13 · "Happily ever after"? No (1:49:46–1:57:00).** Iroha chooses law school. The narration closes the story and an "AND SHE LIVED HAPPILY EVER AFTER" card appears (1:51:30). Iroha refuses that ending (1:51:40). She phones her mother and finally says what she wants (1:52:30–1:52:56). She finishes a song and sings it, but it will not send to Yachiyo. FUSHI leads her to a door and she logs in (1:56:40).

**14 · Yachiyo is Kaguya (1:57:00–2:07:12).** Iroha asks Yachiyo whether she is Kaguya, and Yachiyo tells it as a fairy tale (1:57:34). Back on the moon, Iroha's song reached Kaguya. She raced back through time, but her ship hit a giant rock and she landed about 8000 years in the past. At first she could reach the world only through a sea slug. Once humans built networks she could exist as a soul, so she became Tsukuyomi's singer and found Iroha again. "Your" Kaguya is still going round the same loop. Yachiyo apologises for breaking her promise: "the sparkly princess is an old lady now". Iroha demands all 8000 years of stories. FUSHI then shows her Yachiyo's memories (2:03:11–2:04:38), and Iroha understands why Yachiyo always smiles. Iroha sings "Remember" to her, and Yachiyo says that song kept her alive. Iroha resolves that the story is not over.

**15 · Ten years later (2:07:15–2:20:11).** After a montage, a TEN YEARS LATER card (2:07:49). Iroha is close to a breakthrough on avatar bodies and asks her brother to invest. Kaguya boots up in a physical body and wants pancakes, though taste "isn't ready yet" (2:09:04–2:09:29). Her comeback concert runs in the real world and Tsukuyomi at once, dancing with Yachiyo. Kaguya asks the viewer whether this is a happy ending, and the title appears (2:10:26). End credits: "ray", then "Melt". In a post-credits gag, Yachiyo still wants those pancakes (2:19:55).

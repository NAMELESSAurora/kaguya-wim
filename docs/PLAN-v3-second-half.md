# Second half v3 — space, layers, camera (the user, 2026-11-24)

> "我需要的效果是后半段相较于前半段更具观赏性更具创新和设计感以及运用优质学习样片的内容，这不仅意味着更多素材的运用，
> 还有对空间以及层次感和空间运镜感的极致理解和应用"

The first half is a sequence of designed frames; the second half must be a sequence of designed **spaces** the camera travels
through. Devices from the study references (pv-mad-motion/references/case-space-layers-fsf-baka.md, the Fate/strange Fake MAD and
「バカみたいに」, plus the duotone GMV and the vertical MAD):

- type and objects planted **between** depth planes, with something passing in front of them;
- 5–7 layers of different kinds in every frame, one of them huge and hairline (the ghost);
- frames within frames that carry **captions**;
- real 3D objects as transitions: a card flung at the lens, ring tunnels, a cylinder of type, a grid floor, a room that turns;
- one continuous camera per section (dolly, orbit, roll), never a static frame; foreground elements cross the lens.

## Verse 2 (98.4–115.2) — THE RUNWAY
A runway corridor in depth: a neon grid floor receding to the far end, poster frames standing at both sides (frames within
frames, each with its caption — LOOK 01 · 火鼠の皮衣 …), the lyric of each line standing in the corridor between the posters.
The camera dollies forward the whole verse, passing each poster as its line plays (fly-through); glitter and danmaku cross in
front; the carousel horse gallops along the floor; the house of cards stands at the corridor's end and is knocked toward the lens
on 「手を取って」.

## Pre 2 (115.2–130.9) — THE MEMORY RING
Drums out: the burning robe card at the centre; Yachiyo's first-live windows on a tilted ring around it, front and back; the
camera orbits half a turn round the card, so the windows pass behind and in front of it (one crosses the lens). Embers rise in
three depths. Drums in: the nobori avenue in three rows, the camera weaving between them to the countdown.

## Chorus 2 (130.9–161.4) — THE ROOM OF CARDS
Inside a room whose three walls are card walls showing Yachiyo's first live (left, back, right). The camera stands in the middle
and turns (yaw) on each line, so a new wall faces it; the hook rolls the camera so the floor (lyric lying on it) becomes the view.
HUD held in the lens. Cardistry in the centre of the room: riffle, ribbon, throws crossing between the walls, the fan wheel.

## Bridge (163.5–181.8) — THE POP-UP BOOK
A diorama of cut-out layers at five depths (sweets, frames, the menu, the treasures); the camera trucks sideways across it and each
layer folds UP from flat as the camera reaches it (a pop-up book); 「我慢します」 hairline huge behind everything.

## Final (185.3–210.25) — THE SPHERE AND THE VORTEX
The crown ring fly-through; inside a sphere of cards (all the singers) that turns and flips in waves around the camera, the stage
window ahead; the hug as pure footage; then the sphere collapses into a vortex tunnel of cards with headlight trails racing down
it on 「轢かれる」; ALL CLEAR as the camera exits the tunnel.

## Outro (210.25–226) — LIGHTS OUT IN DEPTH
The grid of cards falls back into the dark one by one as each goes out; the camera pulls back; the last emblem, the moon, the pole.
